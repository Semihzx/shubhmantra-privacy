1. Data Collection & Storage

ShubhMantra is built with a strict local-first architecture. Information you optionally enter in the app—such as your display name, birth date, birth time, birthplace selection, Rashi preference, saved favorite shlokas, and reminder preferences—is stored exclusively on your own device inside local app storage (localStorage and Android SharedPreferences).

We do not operate user-tracking accounts, and we do not collect, upload, sell, rent, or transmit your personal profile or birth details to external servers or third parties. Cloud backup for app files is explicitly disabled (allowBackup="false").

2. App Permissions Explained

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Strictly optional. Used only if you enable morning, Rahu Kaal, evening aarti reminders, or the optional Lock-Screen Kavach display.
  • Foreground Service (specialUse): Runs only when you explicitly turn on the Lock-Screen Kavach toggle in Settings, keeping the home-screen widget and verse rotation active while displaying an ongoing notification.
  • Image Save & Share: When you choose to save or share a 9:16 status card, the generated PNG image is saved to your Pictures folder (Pictures/ShubhMantra) using Android Scoped Storage (MediaStore) or shared via Android’s native share sheet. No photo library read access is requested.

3. Data Deletion Control

You can permanently erase all saved profile data, birth details, and preferences at any time directly inside the app via Shield Icon → Privacy Policy → Erase All Local Profile Data, or via Settings → Reset App Data, or by uninstalling the application from your device.

4. Cultural & Spiritual Guidance Notice

All shlokas, Panchang timings, Choghadiya intervals, Rashifal, and Vedic Janam Kundli charts inside ShubhMantra are derived from traditional Indian Sanatan texts and classical Jyotish calculations for spiritual reflection and cultural heritage purposes only. They do not constitute medical, legal, or financial advice.

5. Age Audience

ShubhMantra is designed for general adult and teen spiritual reading audiences (ages 13+) and is not directed to children under 13.
